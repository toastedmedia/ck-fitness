How it works

Your day ring

One ring shows the whole day. It fills as the member completes things, and water and steps count partially.

Workout done 40%
Water (to daily target) 30%
Steps (to daily goal) 20%
Daily check-in 10%

The split lives in one setting, so CK can change it later without a new app version.

Personal reminders

Up to two bars at the top, addressed by name: weigh-in every 7 days (appears from day 5), weekly check-in, and "pick up where you left off" after 3 quiet days. Members can dismiss them. Wording stays supportive.

Body snapshot

Weight, trend and BMI. BMI is information only and members can hide it. First weigh-in is captured in onboarding.

Apple HealthiPhone app

A website cannot read Apple Health. Until the iPhone app ships, steps are entered by hand. The card shows where Apple Health will connect, so the layout will not change later.

Easy to restyle

Colours, fonts, corner shapes and spacing come from one theme file. New features can be added as extra cards under the ring.
