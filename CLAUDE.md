## Key decisions (with dates)

- **Video on Cloudflare R2, not Stream (13 Oct, owner decision; deviation from the guide; confirmed again 4 Nov when buffering was fixed with our own HLS streaming on R2 instead of moving to Stream):** Stream bills per minute watched; R2 bills storage only and has no viewing fees, no length or size limit. Admin uploads straight to R2 in slices; a GitHub job makes 1080/720/480/360p HLS versions; playback is adaptive (Apple's own player on Apple devices, hls.js elsewhere, starts at about 480p and climbs) with 6-hour signed links after an access check, falling back to the original file. Lesson thumbnails added. `stream_uid` column kept but unused.
- **App Store plan (13 Oct, owner idea):** the store app is FREE; the two free programs are what the app shows. Paid membership is sold on the website (landing page -> sign in -> Stripe at app.chardikalafitness.com/membership); the account then unlocks automatically via the Stripe webhook (no 24-hour wait needed; admin can also grant manually). Inside the native app (user agent contains `CKFitnessApp`, see `lib/native.ts`) there are NO prices, buy buttons, checkout or billing links: Paywall, Membership page and Home card are neutral. The landing page must live outside the app and the app must never link to it. Still a review risk (Apple 3.1.1 / Google Play billing); re-check rules at submission. See Pending. Apple/Google rules on selling digital content outside their billing are a real risk; decision and wording must be re-checked at submission time.
- **Business model for Apple (3 Nov, owner decision; replaces the website-subscription plan above for now):** the studio comes first. The app is a free lead magnet (anyone signs up free on the website or in the app). When someone becomes an active in-person studio client (usually 1-2 sessions a week), CK staff switch on the Member Library trainings by hand (Admin -> member -> Assign manually: Member, or Give access with "CK Membership") as a complimentary value-add for as long as they train with us. Members CANNOT buy it themselves: `flags.selfServeMembership = false` (`lib/flags.ts`) closes `/api/stripe/checkout`, hides the checkout button and price on `/membership` and the Paywall, and the billing button only shows for accounts with a real Stripe renewal date. Stripe code, webhook and portal stay in place. **Owners plan to make it paid later** (set the flag to true, or use In-App Purchase; re-check Apple 3.1.1/3.1.3 and rewrite the Apple answers first).
