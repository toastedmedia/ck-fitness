What 30 days can do for you

Feel the difference this winter

Winter makes it easy to stop moving. This challenge gives you a reason to keep going, and a plan that makes it simple.

  • A routine you actually keepShow up for 30 days and moving becomes part of your week, not a fight with yourself.
  • More energy on dark daysRegular movement helps you feel more awake and less sluggish through the short winter days.
  • Stronger legs and bodyLower-body and full-body strength work for everyday life: stairs, lifting, carrying the kids.
  • A steadier, stronger coreCore stability work that supports your back and your posture.
  • Better staminaConditioning sessions build your fitness step by step, so you can do more without getting tired.
  • No guessworkEvery day tells you what to do: work out, walk or rest. You just follow the plan.

  • Fits a busy lifeTrain at home, on your schedule. No travel, no gym, no waiting for machines.

Questions

Do I need a gym?

No. You can do the whole challenge at home. Most exercises use your bodyweight, and dumbbells are optional for some moves.

Can I join as a beginner?

Yes. Start with movements and a pace you can control. Chanveer shows you how to do each exercise.

Do I work out every day?

No. The 30-day schedule mixes workouts, walking and rest days.

Is there live support?

Yes. Chanveer holds a live video Q&A call once a week, every Saturday at 11 am Vancouver time. Bring your questions about exercises, form or your schedule.
